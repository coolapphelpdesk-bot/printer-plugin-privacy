Privacy Policy

Print Service · last updated August 3, 2026

1. Summary

Print Service (“the app”) is a print service plugin published by Cool App Helpdesk. It lets you print documents and photos from other Android apps to IPP (IPPS), Wi-Fi Direct, and eSCL-enabled printers and scanners.

  • Print jobs are sent only to the printer you choose, over your local network.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server by the developer.

2. Data we collect

The app collects nothing. It has no accounts and no server. Print jobs contain only the content you choose to print from another app.

5. Network activity and third parties

Print Service discovers and communicates with printers on your local network using standard protocols: mDNS/Bonjour (_ipp._tcp, _ipps._tcp, _uscan._tcp), a subnet port scan, IPP/IPPS (RFC 8010/8011), PWG Raster, and eSCL for scanning. When you print, print data is sent directly to the printer on your network. Encrypted IPPS printing is preferred when the printer offers it.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting, and no social or attribution SDKs. Nothing is sent to any internet service.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Temporary print data is cleared after printing completes. Your saved printer list and settings remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Print Service → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. Print connections are local-network only, and IPPS encryption is preferred when the printer offers it. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
